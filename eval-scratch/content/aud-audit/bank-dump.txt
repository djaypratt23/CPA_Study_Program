## aud-at-pre1 [lesson] Best guess: which attestation engagement provides **no** assurance?
   a) Examination
   b) Review
  *c) Agreed-upon procedures

## aud-at-chk1 [lesson] What form of conclusion does a review report under the SSAEs provide?
   a) An opinion that the subject matter is presented fairly
   b) A disclaimer
   c) A list of procedures and findings
  *d) A statement that the practitioner is not aware of any material modifications that should be made
  E: Reviews provide limited assurance, expressed as negative assurance.
## aud-at-chk2 [lesson] Which engagement is **not** permitted for prospective financial information?
   a) Examination
   b) Compilation
  *c) Review
   d) Agreed-upon procedures
  E: AT-C 305 and AR-C: examinations, compilations, and AUPs are allowed; reviews aren't.
## aud-at-01 [practice] A SaaS company wants a report on its security controls for customers and prospects, including a general-use version for its website. Which reports fit?
   a) SOC 1 Type 2
   b) An audit of internal control over financial reporting
   c) A review of financial statements
  *d) SOC 2 for customers, and SOC 3 for the website
  E: SOC 2 (restricted) and SOC 3 (general use) address the Trust Services Criteria, including security.
## aud-at-02 [practice] Which is a precondition for an attestation engagement?
  *a) The criteria are suitable and available to users
   b) The subject matter must be financial
   c) The practitioner must be the entity's auditor
   d) The engagement must be an examination
  E: AT-C 105 preconditions include suitable, available criteria and a responsible party.
## aud-at-03 [practice] In an examination, the engaging party is also the responsible party and refuses to provide a written assertion. What should the practitioner do?

## aud-at-04 [practice] What is the key difference between a financial forecast and a financial projection?

## aud-at-06 [practice] Which criteria characteristic means that the criteria allow reasonably consistent measurements?

## aud-cash-01 [practice] A check for $30,000 transferring cash from Bank X to Bank Y was recorded as a receipt in Bank Y on December 31 but recorded as a disbursement from Bank X on January 2. What is the effect?
   a) Cash is understated by $30,000
  *b) Cash is overstated by $30,000
   c) No effect
   d) Receivables are overstated
  E: Kiting: the receipt is recorded before year-end and the disbursement after, double-counting the cash.
## aud-cash-02 [practice] Which procedure is most effective in detecting kiting?
   a) Reviewing the payroll register
   b) Counting petty cash
   c) Confirming receivables
  *d) Preparing a bank transfer schedule
  E: The schedule compares book and bank dates for both sides of each transfer.
## aud-cash-03 [practice] Why are cash and negotiable securities at multiple locations counted simultaneously?
   a) Because standards prohibit counting on different days

  *c) To prevent the client from shifting assets between locations to cover a shortage

  E: Simultaneous (or controlled) counts prevent the same assets from being counted twice.
## aud-cash-04 [practice] Which procedure detects unrecorded cash disbursements during the year?
  *a) A proof of cash comparing bank disbursements with book disbursements for the period
   b) A standard bank confirmation
   c) Counting cash on hand
   d) Vouching recorded disbursements
  E: A four-column proof of cash reconciles receipts and disbursements, revealing unrecorded activity.
## aud-cash-05 [practice] The standard bank confirmation is primarily designed to confirm:
  *a) Deposit balances and loan balances with the financial institution
   b) Every check written during the year
   c) The client's creditworthiness
   d) Investment fair values

## aud-cash-06 [practice] A client holds a Level 3 investment valued with an internal model. Which procedure is most appropriate?

## aud-cash-07 [practice] A $10,000 outstanding check on the year-end reconciliation never appears on the cutoff statement. Which follow-up is most appropriate?

## aud-cash-09 [practice] A loan agreement requires a $500,000 compensating balance that can't be withdrawn. How should it be presented?

## aud-coc-01 [practice] Which financial interest held by an audit partner on the engagement **does not** impair independence under the AICPA Code?
   a) 100 shares of the client held in a brokerage account
  *b) An immaterial investment in a diversified mutual fund that holds some client stock
   c) Client bonds held in the partner's IRA that the partner directs
   d) A material investment in a mutual fund that holds client stock
  E: Direct → always impairs. Indirect → impairs if material. A mutual fund's holdings are generally indirect interests.
## aud-coc-02 [practice] A covered member obtains each of the following from a client bank, under normal lending terms. Which one impairs independence?

   b) A credit card with a $3,000 balance paid when due
   c) A loan fully collateralized by the member's cash deposit at the bank
  *d) A new home mortgage
  E: Permitted loans: automobile loans and leases, loans fully collateralized by cash deposits, credit cards and overdraft reserves with balances of $10,000 or less, and insurance policy loans. Mortgages are not on the list.
## aud-coc-03 [practice] An audit senior's sister is the client's chief financial officer. The senior owns no client stock. What is the effect on independence?

   b) Impaired only if the sister owns a material interest in the client
  *c) Impaired if the senior participates on the engagement
   d) Impaired for every client of the firm
  E: Close relatives (parents, siblings, nondependent children) impair independence if they hold a key position or have a material financial interest (known to the member) or significant influence.
## aud-coc-04 [practice] Which situation creates a **self-review** threat?

  E: Self-review threats arise when a member must evaluate results of services the member or firm previously performed.
## aud-coc-05 [practice] On March 1, Year 3, a firm is about to issue its report on a client's Year 2 statements. Which unpaid fee impairs independence?
