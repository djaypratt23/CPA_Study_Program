# FAR: coverage against the AICPA Blueprint (effective January 2026)

Read-only evaluation of `content/far` against the FAR section Blueprint. Blueprint tasks are **abbreviated paraphrases** (12 words or fewer), not quotations. Skill tags: R = Remembering and understanding, Ap = Application, An = Analysis (FAR has no Evaluation tasks). ✓ means the task is taught in a lesson **and** assessed by at least one item at or above its Blueprint skill level; ✗ means it is not.

**Method.** Every practice and exam MCQ (434) and every TBS (31) was assigned to the single Blueprint topic it mainly assesses, using stems, TBS titles and row labels (full questions checked where ambiguous). Task coverage may be credited from an item mapped to another topic (for example, the doc-review parts of TBS u12-contingencies credit I.A.7 and III.G). A TBS or analysis-labelled item counts as analysis only when it genuinely reconciles, detects discrepancies or derives a multi-step effect. Repo item labels were not taken at face value: the bank reconciliations (labelled application) are credited as analysis, while the allowance roll-forward x2-02 (labelled analysis) is not.

**Rating rules.** Full = every task ✓ and (20 or more practice MCQs, or 10 or more MCQs plus at least 1 TBS mapped). Thin = some coverage, but fewer than 50% of tasks taught and assessed at any level, or fewer than 5 mapped items. Partial = everything else with 50% or more of tasks taught and assessed (any level), including topics where every task is covered but the depth or skill bar is missed. Missing = no lesson and no items.

**Ratings (35 topics):** Full 10 · Partial 18 · Thin 7 · Missing 0.

## Confidence and caveats

- **Blueprint structure:** high. Task skill levels come from the verified extraction, and 113 tasks across 35 topics were cross-checked by the script.
- **Item classification:** about 90% of items are unambiguous (module and stem point to one topic). About 20 items are borderline and are listed above; moving them would change counts by one or two per topic. Ratings that one move would flip: III.E (Full at 10 MCQs; moving fv-06 back into it would give 11, moving any one item out would drop it to Partial), II.E.3 (exactly 10 MCQs + 1 TBS), I.C.1 vs I.C.2 (gov-10), and I.A.4 (Thin with 2 items).
