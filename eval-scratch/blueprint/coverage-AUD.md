# AUD Blueprint coverage map (Blueprint effective January 2026)

This is a read-only evaluation of the study app (`content/aud/`) against the AICPA CPA Exam Blueprint for AUD. Task wording is abbreviated to about 12 words or fewer; the Blueprint text is AICPA copyright and is not reproduced here. Inputs: the Blueprint structure (60 topic rows, 147 representative tasks), plus the repo export `repo-items-AUD.json` (32 modules, 323 practice and 78 exam MCQs, 20 practice and 7 exam TBS). I read every lesson, MCQ and TBS in full.

**Legend.** Skills: R = remembering/understanding, Ap = application, An = analysis, E = evaluation (the Blueprint level for each task). ✓ = taught in a lesson AND tested by at least one practice/exam MCQ or TBS at or above the Blueprint skill. ◐ = taught and tested, but only below the skill level, or only partly taught. ✗ = not taught, or not tested (see section c). Items in the `lesson` pool (pre-questions and in-lesson checks) are not counted. An item counts as An/E only when it really requires that skill; the repo's own skill labels were not accepted as given (see d.3).

**Rating rules.** *Full*: every task ✓, and ≥ 20 practice MCQs or (≥ 10 MCQs + ≥ 1 TBS) mapped. *Partial*: ≥ 50 % of tasks ✓/◐, or all tasks covered but below the depth or skill bar. *Thin*: some coverage, but < 50 % of tasks, or < 5 mapped items. *Missing*: no lesson content and no items. Thin is applied before Partial, so a fully taught topic with 4 items is still Thin.

## Summary

- **Ratings (60 topic rows):** Full 4 · Partial 23 · Thin 29 · Missing 4.
- **Tasks:** 147 total. 95 (64 %) are covered at their Blueprint skill level. 128 (87 %) are taught and tested at some level. 19 have no real coverage.
- **Strong:** independence (AICPA/SEC/PCAOB), quality management, risk assessment and response, controls testing, sampling, confirmations, substantive tests of details, and financial statement audit reports.
- **Absent:** single audits / Uniform Guidance (3 topics), GAGAS reporting, data structures and preparation (III.A.1), economics (II.B.1), SOX governance, integrated-audit (ICFR) reporting, compliance attestation (AT-C 315), and auditor cognitive bias.
