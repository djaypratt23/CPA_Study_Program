bigIdea:
  what: >-
    For private companies that don't need an audit, SSARS offers three lighter services: preparing statements (no
    report, no assurance), compiling them (a report, no assurance), and reviewing them (limited assurance based on
    inquiry and analytics).
  why: >-
    A bank may be satisfied with a review, and an owner may just need statements prepared. Matching the service to
    the need saves cost, as long as everyone understands exactly how much assurance (if any) is given.
  example: >-
    A dental practice's bank requires reviewed statements. The CPA performs inquiries and analytical procedures,
    obtains a representation letter, and issues a review report stating it isn't aware of any material
    modifications needed.
preQuestions: [aud-ss-pre1]
keyTakeaways:
  - "Preparation (AR-C 70): no report and no assurance; each page states \"no assurance is provided\" (or a disclaimer accompanies it); independence isn't required."
  - "Compilation (AR-C 80): a report is required; no assurance; independence isn't required, but an impairment must be disclosed in the report (reasons may be given)."
  - "Review (AR-C 90): limited assurance; independence is required; procedures are primarily inquiry and analytical procedures; a representation letter is required."
  - "All three require a written engagement letter (signed by the accountant and management)."
  - "Compilation or preparation with substantially all disclosures omitted is allowed if the omission is disclosed and not intended to mislead. Reviews generally require full disclosures."
  - "Known departures from the framework: in a compilation, disclose the departure in the report (AR-C 80). In a review, modify the conclusion under SSARS 25 (AR-C 90): a qualified conclusion if material, an adverse conclusion if material and pervasive, with a basis paragraph describing the departure. In either engagement the accountant may withdraw if management won't correct it."
  - "Review reports: \"we are not aware of any material modifications that should be made to the accompanying financial statements for them to be in accordance with…\"."
citations:
  - source: SSARS — AR-C 60 (General principles), AR-C 70 (Preparation), AR-C 80 (Compilation), AR-C 90 (Review)

## Comparing the services

| | Preparation (AR-C 70) | Compilation (AR-C 80) | Review (AR-C 90) | Audit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Assurance | None | None | **Limited** | Reasonable |
| Report | None ("no assurance" legend on each page) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Independence required? | No | No (disclose impairment) | **Yes** | Yes |
| Key procedures | Prepare from records | Read statements for obvious errors | **Inquiry and analytics**; representation letter | Risk assessment, tests of controls, substantive tests |
| Engagement letter | Required | Required | Required | Required |

## Review procedures

- **Analytical procedures:** compare with prior periods, budgets, and expected relationships; investigate unexpected fluctuations.
- **Inquiries** of management about accounting principles, unusual transactions, subsequent events, fraud, going concern, and significant journal entries.
- **Representation letter** from management (required), dated as of the review report date.
- Reconcile the statements to the underlying records.
- If the accountant becomes aware information is incorrect, incomplete, or unsatisfactory, it performs additional procedures.

  - label: The same CPA is asked to review the statements

    result: Cannot perform the review
  - label: Management won't record depreciation (a known GAAP departure) in compiled statements
    work: Disclose the departure in the compilation report, or withdraw if needed
    result: Report discloses the departure and its effects if known
  - label: The owner wants statements with substantially all disclosures omitted
    work: Allowed for compilations (and preparations) if the omission is clearly indicated
    result: Compilation report includes a paragraph noting the omitted disclosures
insight: The deciding question in SSARS is always "what assurance, if any, is being given?"
