## The Code at a glance

| Part | Applies to | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Part 1** | Members in public practice | Independence, integrity & objectivity, due care, general standards, compliance with standards, accounting principles, confidentiality, contingent fees, commissions, acts discreditable, advertising, form of organization and name |
| **Part 2** | Members in business | Integrity & objectivity, conflicts of interest, subordination of judgment |
| **Part 3** | Other members (retired, between jobs) | Acts discreditable |

**Principles** (aspirational): responsibilities, the public interest, integrity, objectivity and independence, due care, and scope and nature of services.

## The conceptual framework

When no specific rule covers a situation, the member evaluates **threats** and **safeguards**.

| Threat | Example |
|---|---|
| Self-review | Auditing numbers the firm itself prepared |
| Self-interest | A large unpaid fee or a financial interest in the client |
| Advocacy | Promoting the client's shares or representing it in a dispute |
| Familiarity | A long relationship with the client's CFO, or a relative in a key position |
| Management participation | Making decisions for the client, such as approving transactions |
| Undue influence | The client threatens to replace the firm unless it accepts an accounting treatment |
| Adverse interest | Actual or threatened litigation between client and firm |

Safeguards come from the profession (standards, peer review), the client (a strong audit committee), and the firm (quality management, rotating staff). If safeguards cannot reduce a threat to an acceptable level, independence is impaired.

## Who must be independent? Covered members

- Individuals on the attest engagement team, and anyone who can influence the engagement (e.g., partners who supervise the lead)
- Partners and managers who provide **10 or more hours** of nonattest services to the client
- Partners in the **office** where the lead attest partner primarily practices
- The firm itself and its employee benefit plans

## Financial interests and loans

| Interest | Covered member (or immediate family) |
|---|---|
| Direct financial interest (stock, bonds) — any amount | **Impairs** |
| Indirect financial interest (e.g., a diversified mutual fund holding the client) | Impairs only if **material** |
| Loan from a client that is a lender, made under normal terms | **Impairs**, unless it is a permitted loan or grandfathered |

**Permitted loans** (normal terms): automobile loans and leases collateralized by the car; loans fully collateralized by cash deposits at the lender; credit cards and overdraft reserves with balances of $10,000 or less (reduced by the payment due date); and loans against the cash surrender value of an insurance policy. **Not permitted:** a new home mortgage from a client bank.

## Family relationships

- **Immediate family** (spouse, spousal equivalent, dependents) — treated as if they were the covered member.
- **Close relatives** (parents, siblings, nondependent children) — impair independence if the relative holds a **key position** at the client, or has a financial interest that is **material to the relative** and known to the covered member, or that lets the relative exercise significant influence.

## Nonattest services

Permitted for an attest client **only if** the member does **not** assume management responsibilities and the client:

1. designates an individual with suitable **skill, knowledge and/or experience (SKE)** — preferably senior management — to oversee the service;
2. evaluates the adequacy and results of the service; and
3. accepts responsibility for the results.

The member documents its understanding with the client, in writing, before performing the service.

| Management responsibility (prohibited) | Generally permitted if the conditions are met |
|---|---|
| Authorizing or approving transactions | Recording transactions the client has approved |
| Having custody of client assets | Preparing financial statements from the client's trial balance |
| Designing or operating internal control | Recommending control improvements |
| Making hiring decisions | Screening candidates against client-set criteria |

## Fees and other rules

- **Unpaid fees:** fees for services rendered more than one year before the date of the current-year report that are still unpaid impair independence (a self-interest threat).
- **Contingent fees:** prohibited for any client for which the member performs an audit, review, or examination of prospective financial information, or when preparing an original or amended tax return (with limited exceptions).
- **Commissions and referral fees:** prohibited for attest clients; otherwise permitted if disclosed to the client.
- **Confidential information:** not disclosed without client consent, except for subpoenas and summonses, compliance with laws and standards, peer review, and AICPA or state board ethics investigations.
- **Client records:** client-provided records must be returned on request, even if fees are unpaid.
