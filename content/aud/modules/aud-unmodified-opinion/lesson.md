bigIdea:
  what: >-
    The auditor's report leads with the opinion, then explains its basis, what management is responsible for, and
    what the auditor did. The structure is standardized so readers can find the opinion and compare reports.
  why: >-
    Users skim. Putting the opinion first and using fixed headings makes the conclusion unmistakable — and makes
    any deviation from the standard wording a signal worth reading.
  example: >-
    A private company's report opens with "Opinion", followed by "Basis for Opinion", "Responsibilities of
    Management for the Financial Statements", and "Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Financial
    Statements", then the firm's signature, city and state, and date.

keyTakeaways:
  - "Nonissuer (AU-C 700, SAS 134): title with \"independent\"; addressee; Opinion section first; Basis for Opinion (GAAS, independence and ethics, sufficient appropriate evidence); going concern section if applicable; key audit matters if engaged; Responsibilities of Management; Auditor's Responsibilities; other reporting responsibilities; signature, city and state, date."
  - "Management's responsibilities include evaluating whether there is substantial doubt about going concern (where the framework requires it)."
  - "Auditor's responsibilities: reasonable assurance (high, not absolute); professional judgment and skepticism; risk assessment; internal control understanding but no opinion on it; evaluating policies, estimates, and presentation; concluding on going concern; communicating with TCWG."
  - "Issuer (PCAOB AS 3101): Opinion on the Financial Statements; Basis for Opinion (including a statement that the firm is PCAOB-registered and independent); critical audit matters; a statement of the auditor's tenure; signature, city and state, date."
  - "The report is dated no earlier than when sufficient appropriate evidence is obtained."
  - "For comparative statements, the opinion covers each period presented."

## Nonissuer report (SAS 134)

| Order | Section | Key content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | "Independent Auditor's Report" |
| 2 | Addressee | Board, shareholders, or as required |
| 3 | **Opinion** | Identifies the entity and statements; "present fairly, in all material respects… in accordance with [framework]" |
| 4 | **Basis for Opinion** | Conducted under GAAS; independent and met ethical responsibilities; evidence is sufficient and appropriate |
| 5 | Going concern (if applicable) | "Substantial Doubt About the Entity's Ability to Continue as a Going Concern" |
| 6 | Key audit matters (if engaged) | Matters of most significance |
| 7 | **Responsibilities of Management** | Preparation and fair presentation; internal control; going concern evaluation |
| 8 | **Auditor's Responsibilities** | Reasonable assurance; misstatements can arise from fraud or error; skepticism; risks; internal control (no opinion); estimates; going concern; communication with TCWG |
| 9 | Other reporting responsibilities | E.g., supplementary information, other information |
| 10 | Signature, city and state, date | Firm's signature; date sufficient appropriate evidence was obtained |

## Issuer report (PCAOB AS 3101)

| Nonissuer (AICPA) | Issuer (PCAOB) |
|---|---|
| "Opinion" | "Opinion on the Financial Statements" |
| Conducted under GAAS | Conducted under **PCAOB standards**; the firm is registered with the PCAOB and required to be independent |
| KAMs only if engaged | **Critical audit matters** required (with exceptions such as emerging growth companies) |
| No tenure statement | **"We have served as the Company's auditor since [year]."** |
| Management and auditor responsibilities in separate sections | Responsibilities folded into the Basis for Opinion |

    work: Required element of the Basis for Opinion
    result: Correct
insight: Read each sentence against the three anchors — which standards, what assurance, and what the opinion covers.
