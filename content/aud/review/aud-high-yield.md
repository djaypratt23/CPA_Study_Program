## Area I — Ethics, professional responsibilities & general principles (15–25%)

- **Covered members:** the engagement team, people who can influence it, partners and managers with 10+ hours of nonattest services, partners in the lead partner's office, and the firm itself.
- **Direct** financial interest → impaired at any amount. **Indirect** → impaired only if material.
- **Permitted loans:** auto, cash-collateralized, credit cards ≤ $10,000, insurance policy loans. **Not** a new home mortgage.
- **Unpaid fees** for services rendered more than 1 year before the report date → impaired.
- **Nonattest services:** no management responsibilities; the client designates an overseer with suitable SKE, evaluates the results, and accepts responsibility; document the understanding.
- **Issuers:** prohibited services (bookkeeping, systems design, valuation, actuarial, internal audit, HR, legal, broker-dealer, expert services); audit committee pre-approves everything; rotation — lead and EQR partners 5 on / 5 off, other partners 7 on / 2 off; 1-year cooling-off.
- **Quality management (SQMS 1):** eight components; EQ reviewer cooling-off is 2 years.
- **Acceptance:** preconditions — an acceptable framework plus management's acknowledgment of its responsibilities; inquire of the predecessor before accepting.

## Area II — Assessing risk & developing a planned response (25–35%)

- **AR = IR × CR × DR.** Detection risk is the only component the auditor controls; it moves inversely with RMM.
- **Inherent risk factors:** complexity, subjectivity, change, uncertainty, and susceptibility to bias or fraud. A significant risk sits at the top of the spectrum.
- **Analytics are required** in risk assessment and in the final review (substantive analytics are optional).
- **Fraud:** revenue presumed (rebuttable); management override always → test journal entries, review estimates retrospectively, and evaluate unusual transactions.
- **Controls:** COSO (CRIME); segregate authorization, recording, and custody; material weakness = reasonable possibility of a material misstatement not being prevented or detected; SD and MW in writing within 60 days after report release.
- **Tests of controls:** required if relying or if substantive procedures alone are insufficient; inquiry alone is never enough; rotate unchanged controls (test at least every third year), but significant-risk controls must be tested every year.

## Area III — Performing further procedures & obtaining evidence (30–40%)

- **Direction:** existence → vouch (records → source). Completeness → trace (source → records).
- **Confirm receivables** unless immaterial, ineffective, or low RMM with other procedures. Nonresponse → alternative procedures.
- **Inventory:** attend the count; floor → sheets tests completeness, sheets → floor tests existence; consigned-in goods are excluded.
- **Cash:** cutoff statement obtained directly from the bank; the bank transfer schedule catches kiting.
- **Liabilities:** search for unrecorded liabilities using post-year-end disbursements; a lawyer's refusal to respond is a scope limitation.
- **Sampling:** effectiveness risks = assessing control risk too low and incorrect acceptance. MUS interval = BV ÷ n; projection = tainting × interval (actual misstatement for items at or above the interval).
- **Subsequent events:** search through the report date; dual-date or re-date; subsequently discovered facts → evaluate, then a new report or steps to prevent reliance.
- **Going concern:** one year after issuance; plans must be probable; if doubt remains and disclosure is adequate → unmodified opinion + going concern section.
- **Representations:** dated as of the report date; a refusal on management's responsibilities → disclaim or withdraw.

## Area IV — Forming conclusions & reporting (10–20%)

- **Opinion matrix:** misstatement — material → qualified, pervasive → adverse. Scope limitation — material → qualified, pervasive → disclaimer.
- **Emphasis of matter** (in the statements): change in principle, correction of an error, special purpose framework, major uncertainty or catastrophe. **Other matter** (not in the statements): restricted use, predecessor, unaudited prior period.
- **Estimate change** → no consistency paragraph.
- **KAMs** (AICPA, only if engaged) and **CAMs** (PCAOB) never replace a modified opinion.
- **SSARS:** preparation — no report; compilation — report, no assurance, independence not required (disclose); review — limited assurance via inquiry and analytics, independence and a representation letter required.
- **Attestation:** examination (reasonable), review (limited), AUP (findings only). No reviews of prospective financial information; projections are restricted use.
