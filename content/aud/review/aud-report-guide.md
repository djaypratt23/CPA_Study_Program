## Step 1 — Is there a problem with the statements or the evidence?

| Problem | Material, not pervasive | Pervasive |
|---|---|---|
| **Misstatement** (GAAP departure, including missing disclosures) | Qualified — "except for the effects" | Adverse — "do not present fairly" |
| **Can't obtain evidence** (scope limitation) | Qualified — "except for the possible effects" | Disclaimer — "we do not express an opinion" |

**Pervasive** = not confined to specific elements, **or** a substantial proportion of the statements, **or** a disclosure fundamental to understanding.

## Step 2 — Does something need extra attention?

| Situation | Add |
|---|---|
| Substantial doubt remains, adequately disclosed | "Substantial Doubt About the Entity's Ability to Continue as a Going Concern" section |
| Change in accounting principle (material) or correction of a material error | Emphasis-of-matter paragraph (consistency) |
| Special purpose framework | Emphasis of matter on the basis of accounting (plus restricted use for contractual and regulatory bases) |
| Matter disclosed and fundamental to understanding (e.g., major catastrophe) | Optional emphasis-of-matter paragraph |
| Predecessor audited the prior year; report not reissued | Other-matter paragraph |
| Prior year not audited | Other-matter paragraph |
| Restricted use | Other-matter paragraph (or alert) |
| Other information materially inconsistent and not corrected | Describe it in the Other Information section |

## Step 3 — Dates

| Item | Date |
|---|---|
| Auditor's report | When sufficient appropriate evidence is obtained |
| Representation letter | Same as the report date |
| Dual date | "March 1, except for Note X, as to which the date is March 8" |
| Documentation assembly | 60 days after report release (PCAOB: 45) |
| Retention | 5 years (PCAOB: 7) |
