> Tax year 2025, reflecting the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (P.L. 119-21). Amounts marked † came from that law. Confirm them against current exam materials (see REVIEW.md).

## Individuals

| Item | 2025 amount |
|---|---|
| Estimated tax safe harbor | Lesser of 90% of current-year tax or 100% of prior-year tax (110% if prior AGI > $150,000); no penalty under $1,000 due |
| NIIT | 3.8% × lesser of NII or MAGI over $200,000 single / $250,000 MFJ / $125,000 MFS |
| Additional Medicare tax | 0.9% of wages/SE income over the same thresholds; employer withholds over $200,000 |
| Kiddie tax threshold | $2,700 of unearned income |
| SALT cap † | $40,000 ($20,000 MFS), reduced by 30% of MAGI over $500,000, not below $10,000 |
| Qualified charitable distribution | Up to $108,000; age 70½+ |
| $25,000 rental allowance | Phased out by 50% of MAGI over $100,000 (gone at $150,000) |
| Excess business loss | $313,000 single / $626,000 MFJ |
