## Loss limitation gates (in order)

```
Share of loss
  1. Basis (partner outside basis / S shareholder stock + debt basis)
  2. At-risk (§465): cash + property basis + recourse debt + qualified nonrecourse real estate debt
  3. Passive (§469): offset passive income; $25,000 rental allowance with active participation
  4. Excess business loss (§461(l)): above $313,000 / $626,000 → NOL carryforward
= Deductible loss
```

## Schedule M-1

```
Net income per books
+ Federal income tax expense
+ Excess capital losses
+ Income taxed but not booked (prepaid rent)
+ Book expenses not deducted (fines, 50% meals, excess charity, life insurance premiums, excess book depreciation)
− Book income not taxed (municipal interest, life insurance proceeds)
− Deductions not booked (excess tax depreciation)
= Form 1120, line 28 (before NOL and special deductions)
```

## Partner outside basis

```
Beginning basis
+ Contributions (cash + adjusted basis of property)
+ Share of income (including tax-exempt) and increases in share of liabilities
− Distributions (cash, property basis, decreases in share of liabilities)
− Nondeductible, noncapital expenses
− Losses and deductions (not below zero; excess suspended)
```

## S corporation distributions (with accumulated E&P)

```
AAA → tax-free (reduces basis)
Accumulated E&P → dividend
Remaining (OAA / basis) → tax-free
Excess over basis → capital gain
```

## Trust income

```
TI before distribution deduction and exemption
+ Tax-exempt interest (net of allocable expenses)
− Capital gains allocated to corpus
= DNI
Distribution deduction = lesser of distributions or DNI − tax-exempt portion
Trust TI = TI before DD − DD − exemption ($600 estate / $300 simple / $100 complex)
```

## Asset sales

```
§1245 personal property: ordinary = lesser of gain or all depreciation → rest §1231
§1250 real property:
  Individual → unrecaptured §1250 gain (SL depreciation portion) at max 25%
  Corporation → §291: 20% × lesser of gain or SL depreciation = ordinary
Net §1231 gain → ordinary to extent of prior 5 years' net §1231 losses → rest LTCG
Net §1231 loss → ordinary
```

## Like-kind exchange

```
Realized gain = amount realized (FMV received + cash + liabilities relieved − liabilities assumed) − basis
Boot received = cash + net liability relief
Recognized gain = lesser of realized gain or boot received
New basis = FMV of new property − deferred gain
```

## Installment sale

```
Contract price = selling price − liabilities assumed by buyer (not exceeding basis)
Gross profit % = (selling price − adjusted basis − recapture) ÷ contract price
Gain each year = payments received × gross profit %
Recapture → all in the year of sale
```

## Apportionment

```
Three-factor:   (sales % + property % + payroll %) ÷ 3
Double-weighted: (2 × sales % + property % + payroll %) ÷ 4
Single sales:   sales %
State income = apportionment % × business income + nonbusiness income allocated to the state
```
