## Area I — Financial reporting (30–40%)

- **Discontinued operations**: a component disposal that is a *strategic shift*. Show operating results plus the write-down to fair value less costs to sell, **net of tax**, below continuing operations.
- **OCI**: AFS debt unrealized gains and losses, foreign currency translation, pension gains/losses and prior service cost, effective cash-flow hedges. *Not* equity securities (those go through NI).
- **Cash flows (U.S. GAAP)**: interest and dividends **received** and interest **paid** → operating; dividends **paid** → financing. Noncash investing/financing → disclose.
- **EPS**: stock dividends and splits are retroactive; cumulative preferred dividends are subtracted even if undeclared; test antidilution one security at a time, most dilutive first.
- **Consolidation**: control → consolidate; NCI is measured at fair value; eliminate 100% of intercompany balances and unrealized profit, even with an NCI.
- **Governments**: governmental funds use modified accrual and current financial resources (revenue available within ~60 days); government-wide statements use full accrual and economic resources.
- **NFPs**: two net asset classes (with and without donor restrictions); conditional promises are not revenue; contributed services are recognized only if they create or enhance nonfinancial assets or use specialized skills.

## Area II — Select balance sheet accounts (30–40%)

- **Inventory**: FIFO/average → lower of cost and NRV; LIFO/retail → lower of cost or market.
- **PP&E**: capitalize everything needed to get the asset ready for use; razed-building costs go to *land*.
- **Exchanges**: with commercial substance → recognize gains and losses. Without → defer gains (losses are always recognized).
- **Impairment**: two-step test for held-and-used assets; no reversals. Goodwill impairment is one step, capped at goodwill.
- **Investments**: HTM → amortized cost; AFS → FV through OCI; trading → FV through NI; equity securities → FV through NI (or the measurement alternative); 20–50% significant influence → equity method.
- **Bonds**: discount → expense > cash and the carrying amount rises; premium → the reverse.
- **Treasury stock (cost method)**: gains go to APIC–treasury; losses reduce APIC–treasury, then retained earnings.

## Area III — Select transactions (25–35%)

- **Revenue five steps**: contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate → recognize. Principal = gross; agent = net.
- **Variable consideration**: expected value (many outcomes) or most likely amount (binary), subject to the constraint.
- **Deferred taxes**: enacted rates only; valuation allowance when realization is *not* more likely than not; DTAs and DTLs are noncurrent.
- **Leases**: OWNES (ownership, written purchase option, net PV, economic life, specialized) → finance lease. Operating lease → a single straight-line cost.
- **Accounting changes**: principle → retrospective; estimate (including a depreciation-method change) → prospective; error → restate (prior-period adjustment).
- **Contingencies**: probable + estimable → accrue (best estimate, else the minimum); reasonably possible → disclose; remote → nothing (except guarantees). Gain contingencies → never accrue.
- **Subsequent events**: the condition existed at the BS date → recognize; it arose afterward → disclose. SEC filers evaluate through the issuance date; others through the available-to-be-issued date, and disclose that date.
