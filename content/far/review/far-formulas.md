## Area I — Financial reporting

| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| Basic EPS | (NI − preferred dividends\*) ÷ weighted-average common shares |
| \*Preferred dividends | Cumulative: subtract the current year's amount whether or not declared. Noncumulative: only if declared |
| Diluted EPS — convertible bonds | Add back interest × (1 − t); add the shares (if-converted) |
| Diluted EPS — options | Treasury stock method: shares issued − (proceeds ÷ **average** market price) |
| Current ratio | Current assets ÷ current liabilities |
| Quick (acid-test) ratio | (Cash + marketable securities + receivables) ÷ current liabilities |
| Inventory turnover | COGS ÷ average inventory; days = 365 ÷ turnover |
| Receivables turnover | Net credit sales ÷ average receivables |
| Debt-to-equity | Total liabilities ÷ total equity |
| Return on assets | Net income ÷ average total assets |
| Goodwill (acquisition) | Consideration + FV of NCI + FV of previously held interest − FV of identifiable net assets |
| Unrealized intercompany profit | Intercompany gross profit × % still held by the buyer |

## Area II — Balance sheet accounts

| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| Credit loss expense | Required ending allowance − (beginning + recoveries − write-offs) |
| Gross profit method | Ending inventory = goods available − sales × (1 − GP%) |
| DDB depreciation | (Cost − accumulated depreciation) × 2 ÷ life (ignore salvage until the floor) |
| SYD depreciation | (Cost − salvage) × remaining years ÷ (n(n+1)/2) |
| Avoidable interest | Weighted-average accumulated expenditures × rate (specific borrowing first, then the weighted rate on other debt) |
| Impairment (held and used) | Test: carrying amount > undiscounted cash flows? Loss = carrying amount − fair value |
| Goodwill impairment | Reporting unit carrying amount − fair value, capped at goodwill |
| Effective interest | Interest = carrying amount × market rate; amortization = interest − cash |
| Equity method | Cost + share of NI − share of dividends − excess-cost amortization |
| Debt extinguishment | Gain/loss = net carrying amount − reacquisition price |

## Area III — Transactions

| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| Revenue allocation | Price × (SSP of item ÷ total SSP) |
| Cost-to-cost progress | Costs to date ÷ total estimated costs |
| Over-time gross profit | (Progress % × total estimated GP) − GP recognized in prior years |
| Deferred taxes | Temporary difference × **enacted** rate for the reversal year |
| Total tax expense | Current (taxable income × rate) + ΔDTL − ΔDTA (+ Δvaluation allowance) |
| Lease liability | PV of lease payments (annuity due if payments are at the start) |
| ROU asset | Lease liability + initial direct costs + prepaid rent − incentives |
| Operating lease cost | Total payments ÷ lease term (straight-line) |
| Change in estimate | (Carrying amount at the change − new salvage) ÷ remaining life |
