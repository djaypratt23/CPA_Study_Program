> General orientation for the REG (Taxation and Regulation) section. Rules change: confirm the details with your board of accountancy, NASBA and Prometric before test day.

## Before test day

- **Notice to Schedule (NTS).** After your board approves your application you receive an NTS for the section. It is valid for a period set by your jurisdiction; schedule with Prometric within it.
- **Scheduling.** Book early, especially near quarter-ends. Check the reschedule and cancellation deadlines and fees when you book.
- **ID.** Bring the unexpired, government-issued photo ID whose name matches your NTS exactly. A mismatch can mean being turned away.
- **The simulated exams in this app** follow the section's structure below. Take one under timed conditions in the final-review window.

## At the test center

- Arrive about 30 minutes early for check-in: ID check, photo, fingerprint or signature, and a scan.
- Personal items (phone, watch, notes, food) go in a locker. You get the center's noteboard and marker; the calculator and spreadsheet are on screen.
- You accept a confidentiality statement and complete a short introduction on screen. Read the time limit for accepting it.

## The testlet rhythm (REG)

| Testlet | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | 36 multiple-choice questions |
| 2 | 36 multiple-choice questions |
| 3 | 2 task-based simulations |
| 4 | 3 task-based simulations |
| 5 | 3 task-based simulations |

- **Total: 72 multiple-choice questions and 8 simulations in 4 hours.**
- A **standard 15-minute break** is offered after testlet 3; it does not count against your time. Any other break does, and the clock keeps running.
- Once you submit a testlet you **cannot return** to it. Review flagged items before you submit.
- **Pace yourself.** A common plan: about 1.5 minutes per multiple-choice question, and bank the rest of your time for the simulations, which take longer. The results screens in this app compare your pace with these targets.

## Pretest items and scoring

- Each section includes a few **unscored pretest items** (multiple-choice and simulations) that are being trialled. You cannot tell which they are, so treat every item as if it counts.
- Scores are reported on a **0–99 scale; 75 is passing**. The score is not a percent correct: harder questions carry more weight, and multiple-choice and simulation scores are weighted by section.
- Guessing is not penalized — never leave an item blank.

## After the exam

- **Score release.** Scores are released on published score-release dates, in batches, and appear through NASBA or your board. Check the current release schedule rather than expecting a score on a fixed number of days.
- **Credit window.** A passed section counts toward the others for a limited credit period set by your jurisdiction (in most jurisdictions now 30 months). Plan your remaining sections inside it.
- **If you don't pass,** your score report shows relative performance by content area. Use it with this app's analytics to target your weakest areas, then retake when your jurisdiction allows.
