## Form 1040 flow

```
Gross income (all income unless excluded)
− Adjustments (HSA, ½ SE tax, SE health insurance, IRA, student loan interest, educator expenses)
= AGI
− Greater of standard or itemized deductions
− QBI deduction, and 2025–2028 deductions (tips, overtime, car loan interest, senior)
= Taxable income → tax (+ AMT, SE tax, NIIT, additional Medicare)
− Credits (nonrefundable first, then refundable)
= Tax due or refund
```

## Property

| Formula | |
|---|---|
| Gain/loss | Amount realized (cash + FMV + debt relief − selling costs) − adjusted basis |
| Like-kind recognized gain | Lesser of realized gain or boot received |
| Like-kind new basis | FMV of new − deferred gain |
| Gift basis (FMV < donor basis) | Gain: donor basis; loss: FMV; in between: none |
| §1245 | Ordinary = lesser of gain or depreciation |
| Unrecaptured §1250 (individual) | Lesser of gain or SL depreciation → 25% max |
| §291 (corporation) | 20% × lesser of gain or SL depreciation → ordinary |
| Installment gain | Payment × (gross profit ÷ contract price) |

## Corporations

| Formula | |
|---|---|
| Taxable income from book | Book income + federal tax + nondeductibles − exempt income ± temporary differences |
| Current E&P | TI + exempt income + DRD + excess tax depreciation − federal tax − nondeductibles |
| Distribution ordering | Current E&P → accumulated E&P → basis → capital gain |
| §351 stock basis | Property basis + gain − boot − liabilities assumed |

## Flow-throughs

| Formula | |
|---|---|
| Partner outside basis | Contributions + share of income (incl. exempt) + liability increases − distributions − losses − nondeductibles − liability decreases |
| Partnership distribution gain | Cash − outside basis (if positive) |
| S shareholder loss limit | Stock basis + debt basis (direct loans only) |
| S distributions with E&P | AAA → E&P (dividend) → basis → gain |
