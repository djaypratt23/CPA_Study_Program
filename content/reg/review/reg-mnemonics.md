| Hook | Stands for |
|---|---|
| **MY LEGS** | Statute of frauds: Marriage, >1 Year, Land, Executor, Goods ≥ $500, Suretyship |
| **"Dispatch to accept, receive to revoke"** | Mailbox rule |
| **"File or perfect first wins; PMSI equipment gets 20 days"** | Article 9 priority |
| **"90 days, 1 year for insiders"** | Bankruptcy preference look-back |
| **"Reasonable basis + disclose, substantial authority alone, MLTN for shelters"** | Return position standards |
| **"$1K or 50%; $5K or 75%"** | §6694(a) and (b) preparer penalties |
| **"3-6-forever; 3 or 2"** | Assessment statute (3 years, 6 for >25% omission, no limit for fraud); refund claim (3 years from filing or 2 from payment) |
| **"Tax Court — no pay; others — pay first"** | Choosing a forum |
| **"Deferred gain hides in basis"** | In §351, §1031, §721, and §1033 transactions, untaxed gain is preserved through a lower (carryover or reduced) basis |
| **"AAA before E&P"** | S corporation distributions |
| **"Cash > basis = gain"** | Partnership current distributions |
| **"Families of 318: spouse, kids, grandkids, parents"** | Redemption attribution (no siblings) |

## Two-minute self-test

1. A partner's share of tax-exempt interest — does it change basis? *(Yes, it increases it.)*
2. A guarantee of an S corporation loan — does it create debt basis? *(No.)*
3. Can a C corporation deduct a net capital loss? *(No — carry back 3, forward 5.)*
4. Head of household for a parent living in her own apartment? *(Yes, if the taxpayer pays more than half the cost of her home and she's a dependent.)*
