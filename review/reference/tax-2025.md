# Federal Tax Reference Sheet — Tax Year 2025 (with 2026 where known)

Purpose: a reference for reviewers of the REG / TCP content on the CPA study platform. The platform says it teaches **tax year 2025 law, including the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA, P.L. 119-21, signed July 4, 2025)**. Compiled 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-09.

## How this was compiled and what the Status column means

- Direct page fetches (irs.gov, aicpa-cima.com and others) were blocked. Every figure was checked through **web-search result summaries and snippets**. No page body was read in full. Where a primary authority is named (a Rev. Proc., Notice, IRC section or OBBBA section), it is the authority the source cited, or the known statutory home of the rule.
- The shared web-search budget ran out partway through the first pass. This second pass used 20 searches. A few items therefore rest on memory, and they are labelled as such.
- **Status values**
  - `verified`: confirmed by at least one search result from a reasonably reliable source (IRS, CRS/Congress, AICPA, JofA or Tax Adviser, a Big-4 or national CPA/law firm, Thomson Reuters/Checkpoint, Wolters Kluwer, an established CPA review provider, or a major financial publisher). The URL given is the one that confirmed it.
  - `from-memory`: my own knowledge, not confirmed by a reliable source in this session. "(2° agrees)" means a lower-quality secondary site (calculator or blog) showed the same figure.
  - `unverified`: could not be confirmed, or the sources conflict. The conflict is described in the row.
- Abbreviations: RP = Revenue Procedure. RP 2024-40 = 2025 inflation adjustments, issued before OBBBA. RP 2025-32 = 2026 inflation adjustments, which reflect OBBBA. N = IRS Notice. OBBBA § = section of P.L. 119-21.

## 14. Surprises and points for reviewers

1. **Testing scope in Q4 2026.** Read literally, the standard rule makes OBBBA provisions effective 1/1/2026 testable from July 1, 2026, and UWorld lists the $15M exemption as testable from July 1, 2026. A platform that teaches only "2025 law" may be missing testable 2026 statutory changes. This is an inference, so confirm it against the AICPA's *Effective dates of tax provisions in H.R. 1*.
2. **The 2025 additional standard deduction is $1,600 / $2,000.** Several sites still show the 2024 amounts of $1,550 / $1,950.
3. **The §179 increase does not depend on Jan 19, 2025.** It applies to property placed in service in tax years beginning after 12/31/2024. The Jan 19, 2025 date is the acquisition cut-off for 100% bonus depreciation.
4. **The excess business loss threshold falls in 2026** ($313k/$626k in 2025 to $256k/$512k in 2026).
5. **AMT in 2026:** the phase-out begins much lower ($500k/$1M) and the phase-out rate doubles to 50%.
6. **For trusts, $3,150 is the top of the 10% bracket, not the 0% capital-gains breakpoint.** The 2025 0% LTCG breakpoint for trusts is $3,250.
7. **The non-itemizer charitable deduction (2026): placement relative to AGI is disputed.** Secondary sources call it above-the-line. I believe it is a taxable-income deduction under §63(b). Any question that turns on AGI should be checked.
8. **Mortgage insurance premiums** are deductible again only from 2026, not for 2025.
9. **The senior deduction is per person and stacks** with the additional standard deduction for age 65+.
10. **The 2026 refundable CTC is $1,700** (Kiplinger, Newsweek). One secondary source says $1,800.
11. The gift annual exclusion stays at **$19,000** for 2026.
12. **Dependent care in 2026 has a second phase-down** toward 20% above $75k / $150k AGI. Secondary tables disagree on the breakpoints.
