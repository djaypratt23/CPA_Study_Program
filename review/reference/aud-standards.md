# AUD Reference Sheet: Auditing, Attestation, Ethics and Governmental Standards (2026 CPA Exam)

Prepared 2026-10-07, last updated 2026-10-09, for reviewers of AUD study content.

## How to read this sheet

- **Method.** Direct fetches of aicpa-cima.com, pcaobus.org and gao.gov were blocked, so facts come from web-search results. Most of those results quote or summarize primary sources: AICPA, PCAOB and SEC releases, GAO, eCFR, Journal of Accountancy (JofA), Deloitte DART and firm alerts.
- **Search budget ran out partway through.** The shared search budget was used up during section 7. Most of sections 8 and 9, and some rows in sections 4 and 5, could not be checked against a source. Those rows are labelled `from-memory`.

**Status labels**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `verified` | A search result quoted or summarized a primary source (AICPA, PCAOB, SEC, GAO, eCFR, Federal Register) or two or more reputable secondary sources agreed. |
| `verified-secondary` | One reputable secondary source supports it (JofA, DART, a Big 4 or top-25 firm alert, a state CPA society). |
| `partial` | The core fact is supported, but a detail in the row is not. |
| `from-memory` | The author's own knowledge. No source was found this session. Re-check before publishing. |
| `unverified` | Sources conflict or are missing. Do not use as written. |
| `CORRECTED` | The premise in the original question, or a claim common in older study guides, is wrong. The correct rule is stated. |
