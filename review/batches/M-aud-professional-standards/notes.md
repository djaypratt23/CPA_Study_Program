## (b) Lesson design

- **Big idea → pre-question → body → takeaways → checks.** The big idea is clear and correct: reasonable assurance, management's responsibility, and which body issues which standards. There is one pre-question (pre1, management's responsibility). It primes the premise section well but nothing else; no pre-question covers must/should or biases (finding 019, S4). The body has four sections, two tables, a worked example and two inline checks (chk1 standards, chk2 "should"). The key takeaways match the body.
- **Accuracy.**
  - The AS 1000 citation is current. AS 1000 is effective for FY beginning on or after Dec 15, 2024 and was testable from Jan 2025. It supersedes AS 1001, 1005, 1010 and 1015.
  - The premise list, inherent limitations, overall objectives and skepticism definition are correct.
  - **Main defect:** the rule for departing from a "should" requirement is taught as "an alternative achieves the objective" instead of AU-C 200.26 (rare circumstances where the specified procedure would be ineffective). The worked example tells the learner a departure is fine whenever an equally good alternative exists (finding 001, S2).
  - The lesson cites PCAOB Rule 3101 but omits its third category, the responsibility to consider (finding 002).
  - The standards table has three gaps: the Yellow Book row lists the entity as "Governmental" only; broker-dealers are missing from the PCAOB row; AU-C 930 interim reviews are missing (finding 003).
- **Currency.** No dated figures appear in the lesson. All standards cited are in force for 2026: GAGAS 2024, AS 1000, and SAS 146-era bias terminology. There is no taxYear; it does not apply to AUD.
- **Minutes.** 14 minutes for about 1,020 words, 2 tables plus the bias table, 1 worked block and 3 questions. That is realistic, even slightly generous.
- **Lesson–item alignment (finding 004).** These items test material the lesson never teaches:
  - aud-ps-14 and aud-ps-25: AU-C 700 reporting under both GAAS and PCAOB. No AUD lesson teaches this.
  - aud-ps-17 and aud-ps-18: interpretive and other auditing publications. No AUD lesson teaches this.
  - aud-ps-15: inquiry alone is insufficient (taught later, in aud-risk-response).
  - aud-ps-26: competence and specialists (taught later, in aud-quality-management).
  - aud-ps-27: AU-C 585 (taught later, in aud-subsequent-events).
  - aud-x1-12: AU-C 703 and ERISA (taught later, in aud-government-compliance).
  - aud-tbs-r3-documentation: AU-C 230 (taught later, in aud-evidence-assertions, Area III).
  - The distractor "due professional care" in aud-ps-04 is also never taught.
- **Sequencing.** This is the first module of unit aud-u2. It needs only aud-code-of-conduct concepts, which come earlier. The forward dependencies are the items listed above: the module's only TBS, and ps-26/27 and x1-12, rely on later modules. The Blueprint puts documentation in Area I (group E), but the course teaches it in Area III.

- **Counts.** 27 practice MCQs, 3 lesson MCQs, 6 exam MCQs, 1 practice TBS and 6 flashcards. That is plenty for a short framework module. Too many items are redundant, though: about 15 of the 33 MCQs sit in duplicate clusters (finding 011). Two exam items that duplicate each other share mock 3 (finding 012).

- **Skill mix (practice + exam, 33 items).**
  - As labelled: R 15 / A 13 / An 3 / E 2.
  - As I judge it: about R 26 / A 7 / An 0 / E 0.
  - Recall-heavy is expected for a framework module. But the 5 analysis and evaluation labels are false (finding 005, S2), and 7 more are one level too high (finding 010).

- **Routing.** Misses on ps-14/17/18/25 route back to a lesson that has nothing on those topics (finding 004).
