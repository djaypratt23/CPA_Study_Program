## (b) Lesson design

- **Big idea.** It is clear and well motivated: the two preconditions, written terms and the predecessor inquiry. The example (an auditor fired over a revenue dispute) primes the predecessor topic well.
- **Pre-question.** `aud-ea-pre1` asks whom the successor asks for permission. It primes the predecessor-communication concept. It doesn't prime the preconditions, which open the body, but that is acceptable.
- **Body.** It has a mermaid flowchart (accurate to AU-C 210.06 and .08), the AU-C 210.07 scope-limitation rule, an engagement-letter table (the required column matches AU-C 210.10(a)–(f)), a predecessor timeline and a worked change-of-engagement example. Its three cases are correct, and its insight (no reference to the original engagement) matches AU-C 210 application material and SSARS.
- **Takeaways.** They are accurate, but the predecessor-inquiry list is the old AU 315 / AS 2610 list. It omits SAS 147's explicit fraud/NOCLAR requirement and the AS 2610.09 related-party item (finding 004). The change-of-engagement takeaway leaves out the stage-of-completion factor (finding 012).
- **Inline checks.** `chk1` (preconditions) and `chk2` (change of engagement) are well placed. The `chk2` stem's "confirm balances with the client's lawyer" is inaccurate (finding 011).
- **Minutes.** The value is 15. The lesson has about 448 frontmatter words and 515 body words (about 960 in all), plus 1 pre-question, 2 checks, a flowchart, a table, a timeline and a 3-step worked example. That comes to about 10–12 minutes. 15 is generous but acceptable.
- **Lesson–item alignment** (finding 013). Items test material the lesson doesn't teach:
  - firm-level acceptance under SQMS 1 and AU-C 220 (competence, capacity, independence, integrity): aud-ea-09, -13, -14, -17, -20;
  - a predecessor's non-response: aud-ea-16;
  - the consent-and-acknowledgment letter: aud-x1-16 (d), aud-ea-03 explanation;
  - what makes a framework acceptable: aud-ea-07;
  - the reaudit no-reference rule: aud-ea-19 (only indirect);
  - the "misunderstanding about the nature of the service" basis for a change: flashcard f5 (not in the lesson);
  - x11 rows s3–s5 (EQR cooling-off, monitoring, fee concentration). These are taught in aud-quality-management and aud-code-of-conduct; moduleIds omits code-of-conduct (finding 015).
- **Sequencing** (finding 017). The lesson and its items use "disclaimer" and "qualified opinion" before aud-modified-opinions (AUD-IV). "Review" is defined in the earlier aud-professional-standards lesson, so it is fine. No other reliance on later modules.
- **Citations.** AU-C 210, AU-C 510 and AS 2610 are correct and current. The lesson should also name SAS 147 (finding 004). SQMS 1 and AU-C 220 should be cited if a firm-level acceptance section is added.

## (c) Practice design

- **Counts.** 27 practice MCQs, 3 lesson MCQs, 5 exam MCQs, 2 practice TBS and 1 exam TBS. That is ample for a module that maps to Blueprint AUD-I.D (2 topics, 4 tasks).
- **Difficulty** (practice): 1 = 8, 2 = 12, 3 = 7. Items 01–13 progress roughly from easy to hard. Items 14–27 look like a second, later batch (terse 3–7-word options, thin explanations) and restart at difficulty 1 at aud-ea-15 and aud-ea-22. The difficulty-3 items are not actually harder than the difficulty-2 ones (finding 002).
- **Skill mix** (practice and exam, as labeled): remembering 12, application 13, analysis 4, evaluation 3. The labeled analysis and evaluation items are single-rule recall or application (finding 002). On a corrected basis, the module has about 15 remembering, 17 application and 0 analysis or evaluation items. That fits a module on Blueprint tasks labeled R and A (AUD-I.D.1.1 R, D.1.2 A, D.2.1 R, D.2.2 A), but the TBS labels overstate the module's share of the AUD analysis and evaluation bands.
- **Redundancy.** There are four MCQ clusters plus a TBS overlap (finding 005). The exam/practice twins aud-x1-15/aud-ea-23 and aud-x1-23/aud-ea-21 were already filed by C-duplicates (F-0187). aud-x1-31/aud-ea-26 was not, so it is filed here.
- **Cues.** I judged each cues.md flag. The real ones are the "only non-Accept option" pattern (chk1, -24, -26, x1-31), absolutes only in the distractors (-13, -16, -17, -27) and the word match in -09 (finding 006). The other "key longest" and "absolutes" flags (pre1, 01, 02, 03, 05, 11, 15, 18, 19, 21, 25, x1-16, x1-24) are within 2–8 characters or harmless, so they are not filed.
- **Routing.** All module items carry moduleId aud-engagement-acceptance. The x11 moduleIds leave out aud-code-of-conduct (finding 015).

## (e) Needs expert verification

- Finding 004: whether AU-C 210 (as amended by SAS 147) presents integrity, disagreements and the reasons for the change as application guidance ("may include") rather than requirements. The SAS 147 fraud/NOCLAR requirement and its effective date are verified (reference sheet §2; AICPA SAS 147 page; DART). The search did not return the codified AU-C 210 text.
- Finding 012: the exact AR-C 90 wording of the stage-of-completion consideration ("substantially complete … consider the propriety of accepting a change"). I'm confident it appeared in the pre-clarity AR 100, and believe it carries forward into AR-C 90.
- Finding 011: the AR-C 90 point that a client's prohibition on corresponding with legal counsel ordinarily precludes a review report. This is from memory and long-standing SSARS guidance.
- x11 s3: that SQMS 2 makes the 2-year cooling-off a requirement (ISQM 2 puts it in application material). The key is consistent with the aud-quality-management lesson and that batch's review, so it is not filed.
