## TBS aud-tbs-u10-attestation  [pool: practice; 20 min; Attestation and special reports — Whitfield & Co.]
**Instructions:** Whitfield & Co. (a CPA firm) receives the requests below. Select the most appropriate engagement or report feature, then identify the governing guidance for one request.
### Exhibit: Requests
1. A cloud payroll provider wants a report for its customers' auditors on controls relevant to customers' financial reporting, including operating effectiveness over a year.
2. A hospital system wants a publicly posted report on its data security controls.
3. A lender wants specific procedures performed on a borrower's inventory records, with the findings reported — no opinion.
4. A startup wants a CPA's report on its "what-if" statements assuming it wins a major contract, for use in negotiations with one investor.
5. A private partnership wants its tax-basis statements audited for its members.
6. A landlord wants an audit of a tenant's gross sales, which determine percentage rent.
### Part eng (dropdown): Select the best-fitting engagement or report.
Options (shared): SOC 1 Type 2 examination | SOC 3 examination | Agreed-upon procedures engagement | Examination of a financial projection (restricted use) | Audit of special purpose (tax-basis) statements | Audit of a specific element (AU-C 805)
- [e1] 1. Payroll provider — controls relevant to financial reporting, operating effectiveness
- [e2] 2. Publicly posted security report
- [e3] 3. Specified procedures with findings only
- [e4] 4. "What-if" statements for one investor
- [e5] 5. Tax-basis statements
- [e6] 6. Tenant's gross sales
### Part res (research): Which excerpt (paraphrased for study) explains why request 4 must be restricted in use?
- [a] AT-C 305 (paraphrase): A financial projection is appropriate only for the responsible party and parties negotiating directly with it; the practitioner's report on a projection should include an alert restricting its use.
- [b] AU-C 800 (paraphrase): The auditor's report on special purpose financial statements should include an emphasis-of-matter paragraph that alerts users to the basis of accounting.
- [c] AT-C 215 (paraphrase): The engaging party should acknowledge that the agreed-upon procedures are appropriate for the intended purpose of the engagement.
- [d] AU-C 805 (paraphrase): The auditor should determine materiality for the single financial statement or specific element being reported on.
