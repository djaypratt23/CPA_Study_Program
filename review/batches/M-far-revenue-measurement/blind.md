## TBS far-tbs-u9-construction  [pool: practice; 18 min; Long-term contract with a loss year — Summit Builders]
**Instructions:** Summit Builders is constructing a distribution center on the customer's land for a fixed price of **$12,000,000**. Summit concludes revenue is recognized over time and measures progress with the **cost-to-cost** method. Enter losses as negative numbers.
### Exhibit: Contract data
| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cumulative costs incurred | 3,000,000 | 7,200,000 | 12,400,000 |
| Estimated costs to complete | 6,000,000 | 5,300,000 | 0 |
### Part gp (numeric): Compute the gross profit (loss) recognized each year.
- [y1rev] Year 1 revenue
- [y1] Year 1 gross profit
- [y2] Year 2 gross profit (loss)
- [y3] Year 3 gross profit (loss)
### Part res (research): Which excerpt (paraphrased for study) best supports recognizing Summit's revenue over time?
- [a] ASC 606-10-25-30 (paraphrase): If a performance obligation is not satisfied over time, the entity satisfies it at a point in time, considering indicators such as a present right to payment, legal title, physical possession, and customer acceptance.
- [b] ASC 606-10-25-27(b) (paraphrase): An entity transfers control over time if its performance creates or enhances an asset (for example, work in process) that the customer controls as the asset is created or enhanced.
- [c] ASC 606-10-32-8 (paraphrase): An entity estimates variable consideration using either the expected value or the most likely amount, whichever better predicts the consideration to which it will be entitled.
- [d] ASC 340-40-25-1 (paraphrase): The incremental costs of obtaining a contract with a customer are recognized as an asset if the entity expects to recover them.
