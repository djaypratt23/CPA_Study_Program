1. **Receivables:** positive confirmations sent to 40 customers covering 70% of the balance; 34 replied directly to the auditor with no exceptions. For the 6 nonresponses, the auditor examined subsequent cash receipts and shipping documents with no exceptions.
2. **Inventory:** turnover of the outdoor-furniture line fell from 6.1 to 2.3. The auditor obtained an email from the sales manager stating the items are "fully saleable at list price."

4. **PP&E additions:** the audit program called for vouching 25 of 45 additions. The staff vouched 3 before the deadline, with no exceptions.
5. **Cash:** a bank confirmation was received directly; the auditor tested the reconciliation and traced reconciling items to the cutoff bank statement.
