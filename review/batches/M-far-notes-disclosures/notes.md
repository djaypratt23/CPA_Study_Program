## (b) Lesson design

- **Big idea:** clear, concrete and motivating (the founder's trust leases headquarters at below-market rent).
- **Pre-question:** far-nd-pre1 primes the policies-note "how vs. how much" idea, which is the first section. Only one pre-question for four topics. Related parties, the most-tested topic, are never primed (S4 finding).
- **Body:**
  - Accurate on ASC 235 (policies note), ASC 850 (definition, the four disclosures, exclusions, arm's-length rule) and ASC 275 (four areas, concentration criteria, "customers always reasonably possible").
  - Gaps: going concern is absent (S2), as is the ASC 850-10-50-6 control-relationship rule (S3).
  - Imprecise or incomplete: immediate families of principal owners (S3), the labor and foreign-operations concentration disclosures (S3), the wording on changes in terms (S4) and the sign in the interest-paid formula (S4).
  - The "Tying the notes to the statements" section aligns well with the TBS.
- **Takeaways:** accurate. They don't mention going concern, interim reporting or commitments, which the items test.
- **Inline checks:** 2 (policies note; supplier concentration). None on related parties or the tie-out section.
- **Minutes:** 12. The body is about 670 words (about 1,040 with frontmatter), plus a table, a flowchart and 2 checks: about 4 minutes of reading, 3–4 minutes of checks and diagram, about 10 minutes in all. Realistic as is, but going concern needs about 3 more minutes once added.
- **Lesson–item alignment.** Items that test something the lesson never teaches:
  - nd-12, nd-13, nd-17, nd-24: going concern (ASC 205-40)
  - nd-14: ASC 440 unconditional purchase obligations, taught only in later modules (far-debt-other u8, far-contingencies u12)
  - nd-19: ASC 850-10-50-6 control relationship without transactions
  - nd-25, nd-26, nd-27: interim reporting (ASC 270 / 740-270), taught in no FAR lesson
  - nd-28: ASC 250-10-50-4 change-in-estimate disclosure, taught in no lesson

  That is 10 of 28 practice items (36%).
- **Sequencing:**
  - The lesson relies on terms taught later (equity method, u7; consolidation eliminations, u3; debt maturities, u8), but explains each well enough in place.
  - Items nd-14 and nd-28 depend on later modules (u8, u12).

## (c) Practice design

- **MCQs:** 28 practice and 2 exam, 3 lesson-pool. The count is adequate for the module's scope.
- **Labeled difficulty:** 1 = 9, 2 = 7, 3 = 14.
  - Most of the 3s (nd-17 to nd-28) are one-line recall, so the effective spread is about 20 easy, 9 medium and 1 hard (nd-08).
  - Order: nd-01 to nd-16 are mixed 1/2/3; nd-17 to nd-28 are all labeled 3 but are actually easy. There is no real easy-to-hard progression.
- **Skill mix:**
  - As labeled: remembering 9, application 8, analysis 13.
  - As judged: about 16 remembering, 13 application, 1 analysis (nd-08, arguably).
  - FAR Blueprint allocation: remembering 5–15%, application 45–55%, analysis 35–45%. The MCQs are heavily recall. Only the TBS (analysis, correctly labeled) covers the analysis objective.
  - The mislabels (S2 and S3 findings) hide this in any skill-mix tally.
- **Redundancy:**
  - Policies note: nd-11 and nd-22 are identical; pre1, chk1 and nd-20 test the same fact (S3).
  - Going concern "alleviated": nd-13, nd-17 and nd-24 (S3).
  - Lighter overlap, not flagged: nd-10 and nd-21 (related-party disclosure elements), nd-01 and nd-16 (15% shareholder), and nd-08, nd-23 and chk2 (concentration), which use different facts.
- **Mock-form overlap:** far-x1-12 (mock forms 1 and 2) and far-x1-31 (mock form 3) test the same compensation/loan rule but never appear on the same form, so neither cues the other.
- **TBS:**
  - Well built. The footing trap (y5 understated and "thereafter" overstated by the same 40,000) teaches the lesson's point. 11 cells, 5 in error, default tolerance 1, scoring fair, a perfect response scores 100%.
  - Minor: the exhibit doesn't rule out capitalized interest or discount amortization, and 20 minutes is generous (S4).
