## TBS aud-tbs-x12-control-deviations  [pool: exam; 20 min; Evaluate tests of controls — Lindqvist Distributors]
**Instructions:** You tested three controls over Lindqvist's purchasing cycle using attribute sampling. The upper deviation rates come from the firm's evaluation tables. Evaluate each control.
### Exhibit: Test results
| Control | Sample size | Deviations | Upper deviation rate | Tolerable rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A. Purchase orders approved before ordering | 60 | 0 | 4.9% | 6% |
| B. Three-way match before payment | 60 | 2 | 10.3% | 7% |
| C. Vendor master file changes approved | 50 | 1 | 9.2% | 10% |

The deviation in control C was a change to a vendor's bank account made and approved by the CFO alone, contrary to policy.
### Part rates (numeric): Compute for control B.
- [sdr] Sample deviation rate (%)
- [asr] Allowance for sampling risk (%)
### Part ev (dropdown): Evaluate each control.
- [a] Control A  — options: Rely as planned | Do not rely; increase substantive testing
- [b] Control B  — options: Rely as planned | Do not rely; increase substantive testing
- [c] Control C — quantitative result  — options: Within the tolerable rate | Exceeds the tolerable rate
- [cq] Control C — overall conclusion  — options: Rely as planned, because the rate is within the tolerable rate | Weigh its nature: possible override and fraud risk | Ignore it, because only a single deviation was found in the sample
