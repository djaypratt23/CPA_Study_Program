# U-journeys — usability review, journeys 1–8

Setup: `dist/` as built at HEAD, served by `vite preview --port 4173 --strictPort` (no rebuild). Driven with the global Playwright install (Chromium 1194, headless) at desktop 1366×900 and phone 390×844 (isMobile, hasTouch, DPR 2). A fresh browser context per journey; console errors, page errors and failed requests were captured per journey into `logs/*.log` (and full stdout of the mock run in `logs/j6-run.txt`). Scripts: `scripts/lib.cjs` (helpers), `scripts/jN*.cjs` (one per journey), `scripts/tbsfill.cjs`, `scripts/tbs_perfect.ts`, `scripts/findings_list.py` → `findings.jsonl`. Screenshots: `review/screens/uj-*.png`. The session was interrupted several times by usage limits and a worker restart; each time the preview server was restarted on the same port from the same `dist/`, and the journey in progress was re-run from a fresh context.

Answers were chosen from the content JSON (`[data-choice]` ids), so "right" and "wrong" answers are deliberate.

## Journey 1 — first visit, onboarding, first dashboard
Did: fresh context → `#/` redirects to `#/welcome` (about 0.2 s). Step 1 "How this works" → "Got it"; step 2 picked FAR first, looked at the discipline list, typed a past date (2026-01-01), Next, Back (date kept), then 2027-01-15; step 3 kept the default weekly minutes (8 h 30 min) → "Build my plan". Both viewports.
Worked: the gate redirect, three short steps, Back keeps values, the dashboard countdown ("100 days to FAR", long-form exam date), the diagnostic call-to-action, one "Study next" card, today's plan sized to the day's minutes, honest "Not enough data yet" readiness. No horizontal overflow on either viewport.
Didn't: the discipline list shows "BAR — BAR" / "ISC — ISC" (no content for those sections) — U-journeys-001. The date field has no `min` and accepts a past date without comment (consequences covered in journey 7). The status line mixes an ISO date ("finishes by 2026-11-22") with long-form dates elsewhere (cosmetic, not filed). `navigator.storage.persisted()` is false in headless Chromium (expected there; Settings shows it).
Screens: uj-j1-{d,m}-01…04.

## Journey 2 — course, full lesson, inline questions, highlights, notes, complete
Did: Course → "Income statement, OCI & discontinued operations" (pre-question, 2 checks, mermaid, 2 worked, 1 faded). Answered the pre-question wrong with Guess, check 1 right with Confident, check 2 wrong with Unsure; stepped through the worked example; faded example with 4 right (typed "320,000", "$150,000", "100000", "87000") and 1 wrong; selected body text → "Save highlight"; typed a note; reloaded; marked complete.
Worked: inline feedback with per-choice trap labels and explanations; faded example accepts $, commas; mermaid renders (1 svg); notes and highlights survive reload; scroll position is restored on reload (3030 → 2717 px of 8476); dashboard "Continue lesson" points back; after completion the warm-up hides and the module is "Learning" in the course list; Notes page lists the note and highlight.
Didn't: the completion button says "(28 questions)" but starts a 10-question set (U-journeys-002); inline-question highlight tools share one global highlight so a second one erases the first (U-journeys-003); saved highlights are not shown in the text, and text outside the body cannot be saved (U-journeys-004). After a reload the pre-question shows as unanswered again (the attempt was recorded; not filed).
Screens: uj-j2-{d,m}-01…11, uj-j2b-{d,m}-01…03.

## Journey 3 — module practice, mixed set, review queue
Did: tutor set from lesson completion: Q1 right/Confident (auto-advance after 1.5 s works), Q2 wrong/Unsure via keyboard (D then 2) + error tagger ("Concept gap" → "Logged."), Q3 right/Guess ("Correct — but you guessed…", no auto-advance), Q4 flag + note + strike-out, then reload (same question, flag kept, clock kept, 3/10 answered), Q4 right/Unsure, Q5 skipped, Q6–Q10 with one wrong. Results → reviewed a missed item. Mixed set (20) with every confidence rating. Custom Area I set, 4 questions, test mode, timed (06:00 shown = 4 × 90 s). Forced SRS items due (IndexedDB edit + reload) → Review → 18 due questions → answered half right. Flagged filter in the builder found the flagged item.
Worked: confidence prompt is clear; explanations show per choice; flags and notes persist; resume after reload is exact; mixed set from studied modules; test-mode navigator; review queue builds from missed/low-confidence items; pacing line on results.
Didn't: results undercount what entered review and say "Clean sweep" at 50% (U-journeys-005); test-mode "answered" count ignores confidence-less answers and Submit has no confirmation (U-journeys-006); a very fast letter-then-digit keypress drops the confidence (U-journeys-007, S4). The "Mixed practice" card promises interleaving, but with one studied module all 20 come from that module (expected given the data; not filed).
Screens: uj-j3-{d,m}-01…14.

## Journey 4 — flashcards
Did: Review page before any lesson ("0 new", "New cards unlock as you complete lessons"); completed a lesson → "Study 8 cards"; flipped with Space, tap and the button; graded Again/Hard/Good/Easy by click and by keys 1–4; reloaded mid-session; finished; deck page with `?module=`; studied an unstudied module's deck; tried to leave via the nav and Escape.
Worked: interval previews on the grade buttons (1m/6m/10m/8d); Again re-queues within the session; grades persist across a reload; done screen; deck counts.
Didn't: no way to end a session; the "Flashcard decks" nav link leaves you on the card (U-journeys-008); cards graded Again/Hard/Good vanish until their minutes pass, with no hint they return today (U-journeys-009, S4).
Screens: uj-j4-{d,m}-01…07.

## Journey 5 — TBS of each part kind
Did, on both viewports, a perfect and a partially wrong run of: far-tbs-r3-deferred-tax (numeric incl. a % row + journal), aud-tbs-r3-conduct-rules (dropdown), aud-tbs-r3-rep-letter (docreview), far-tbs-u1-income-statement (numeric + docreview + research, using the excerpt search), far-tbs-u1-statement-review (three review parts). Reloaded mid-TBS (numeric "455,000" and the journal account kept; clock kept).
Scoring: every perfect run scored 100% (10/10, 7/7, 4/4, 12/12, 12/12). Partial runs scored exactly the injected mistakes: 8/10 (one numeric, one journal amount), 6/7, 3/4 (kept a wrong original), 9/12 (numeric, docreview, research), 11/12 (flagged a correct review row). Wrong cells show "You: … · Answer: …" with the cell's explanation. Off-UI check: `scripts/tbs_perfect.ts` scored a perfect, UI-typed response (commas, "%" suffix, blank spare journal lines) for all 240 TBS through the app's own `Tbs` schema and `scoreTbs` — 240/240 at 100%, and every keyed dropdown/docreview answer and journal account is offered in its list.
Didn't: bare "1…7" row labels with situations in an exhibit tab make the phone flow a tab shuffle, and the results don't say which situation "1" was (U-journeys-010; 4 TBS); submit scrolls past the score and Submit/Try-again have no confirmation (U-journeys-011, S4).
Screens: uj-j5-{d,m}-<tbs-id>-*.png, uj-j5-{d,m}-00/99 (list).

## Journey 6 — full mock exam (FAR Simulated Exam 1)
Did (desktop): exam home → Start. Testlet 1: answered by click and by keyboard, ArrowRight navigation, flagged Q3, struck out a choice, highlighted stem text, calculator (1234 × 5 = 6170; 100 ÷ 3 = 33.3333333333; Backspace; Escape closes), spreadsheet (=SUM(A1:A2)*0.25 → 1,000), jumped to Q10 via the navigator, reloaded (clock 3:59:55 → 3:59:51, still on Q10, 4 answers and the flag kept), answered all 25 (80% right) → confirm → submit. Browser Back goes to `#/exam`; returning shows testlet 2 (testlet 1 locked). Testlet 2: 20 of 25 answered (60% right), submitted. Testlet 3: TBS 1 perfect, TBS 2 with two wrong cells; switching TBS tabs keeps entries. Break offer → clock stopped; Take break → break timer 14:59 → 14:56 while the exam clock stayed 3:59:21; reload during the break keeps it; Resume → clock runs. Testlet 4: one TBS perfect, two blank. Testlet 5: one TBS perfect → "Submit exam".
Results: "≈ 68; MCQ 64% · TBS 55% · weighted 59%". Checked by hand: MCQ 32/50 = 64% (matches); weighted (64 + 54.5)/2 = 59.3%; the app's mapping 59.3/65 × 75 = 68.4 → 68 (matches). By-area rows with MCQ/TBS splits, pacing per testlet, every item reviewable with explanations, "Add missed questions to spaced review", past attempts listed on the exam home.
Phone: header fits (no overflow), navigator wraps to 3 rows, calculator and spreadsheet overlays measured; a second tab on the same exam shows "This exam is open in another tab" in the older tab with "Continue in this tab"; "Save & exit" → exam home shows "Resume exam in progress".
Didn't: testlet submit gives no unanswered/flagged summary (U-journeys-012); fixed calculator/spreadsheet overlays cover the item on phones, no Backspace, state lost on close (U-journeys-013); last-testlet confirm wording (U-journeys-014, S4); the clock is paused on the break-offer screen too (U-journeys-015, S4); highlights/strike-outs reset when moving between items (U-journeys-016, S4). Minor, not filed: by-area rows are in insertion order (II, III, I).
Screens: uj-j6-d-01…15, uj-j6-m-01…05.
