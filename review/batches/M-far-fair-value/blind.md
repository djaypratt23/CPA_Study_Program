## TBS far-tbs-u10-fair-value  [pool: practice; 15 min; Fair value measurements — Prairie Grain Cooperative]
**Instructions:** Prairie Grain Cooperative reports several items at fair value at December 31. Determine the fair value hierarchy levels and compute the fair value of its wheat inventory held for trading.
### Exhibit: Items measured at fair value
1. **Listed equity securities** — valued at NYSE closing prices.
2. **Interest rate swap** — valued with a model using observable LIBOR-replacement (SOFR) forward curves.
3. **Investment in a private agritech startup** — valued with a DCF using management's revenue projections and a discount rate derived from internal estimates.
4. **Corporate bonds** — valued using quoted prices for similar bonds, as the specific bonds trade infrequently.
### Exhibit: Wheat inventory (100,000 bushels)
| Market | Price per bushel | Transaction cost per bushel | Transport cost per bushel | Share of Prairie's historical volume |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Chicago exchange | 6.20 | 0.30 | 0.40 | 85% |
| Local elevator | 6.00 | 0.05 | 0.10 | 15% |
### Part lvl (dropdown): Select the fair value hierarchy level for each item.
Options (shared): Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3
- [l1] 1. Listed equity securities
- [l2] 2. Interest rate swap
- [l3] 3. Private startup investment
- [l4] 4. Corporate bonds
### Part wheat (numeric): Wheat inventory.
- [pp] Fair value per bushel
- [tot] Total fair value of the wheat
### Part res (research): Which excerpt (paraphrased for study) explains why the Chicago exchange price is used even though the local elevator would net more per bushel?
- [a] ASC 820-10-35-5 (paraphrase): A fair value measurement assumes the transaction takes place in the principal market for the asset or, in the absence of a principal market, in the most advantageous market.
- [b] ASC 820-10-35-9B (paraphrase): The price in the principal (or most advantageous) market is not adjusted for transaction costs, but is adjusted for costs to transport the asset to that market if location is a characteristic of the asset.
- [c] ASC 820-10-35-37 (paraphrase): Inputs are categorized into three levels, giving the highest priority to quoted prices in active markets for identical assets.
- [d] ASC 825-10-25-4 (paraphrase): The fair value option may be elected on an instrument-by-instrument basis and is irrevocable.
