## TBS reg-tbs-u2-contracts-agency  [pool: practice; 25 min; Contracts and agency — Linden Supply]

3. Linden mailed an acceptance of a supplier's offer on June 3; the supplier's revocation, mailed June 2, arrived June 5.

- [s2] 2. Oral oven sale  — options: Enforceable — it's under $5,000 | Unenforceable under the statute of frauds (goods of $500 or more) unless an exception applies | Enforceable because Linden is a merchant
- [s3] 3. Acceptance vs. revocation  — options: A contract formed on June 3 | No contract — the revocation was mailed first | No contract — acceptance is effective on receipt
