## (b) Lesson design

- **Big idea.** It is clear and correct: the condition existed at year-end → adjust; it arose after → disclose. The bankruptcy vs. warehouse-fire example is good.
- **Pre-question (far-se-pre1).** It primes the Type II side (fire → disclose), which is appropriate. It is the same scenario as practice item se-16 (a January 20 warehouse fire), so practice will feel repetitive.
- **Body.** It has a timeline, a decision flowchart and a 9-row Type I/II table. There is an evaluation-date table and a faded two-step example; I recomputed it (+120,000 settlement; flood 0, both correct). There are two inline checks: chk1 (classification) and chk2 (private-company date). All are accurate per ASC 855-10-25-1/25-1A/25-2/50-1 and 55-1/55-2, and ASC 260-10-55-12.
- **Takeaways.** They are accurate. There is one point on pro forma data.
- **Gaps (finding 010).**
  - Reissued statements (855-10-25-4, 50-4/50-5) are missing.
  - The content of a nonrecognized-event disclosure (50-2) is missing, even though objective 2 promises "required disclosures".
  - The definitions of "issued" and "available to be issued" (855-10-20) are missing.
  - Conduit bond obligors (25-1A(b)) are missing.
  - There is no GASB 105 contrast for governments (finding 012, S4).
- **minutes = 12.** That is realistic: about 450 body words plus two tables, a flowchart, a faded example and three questions comes to roughly 9–12 minutes.
- **Lesson–item alignment.** These items test something the lesson never teaches:
  - **far-se-15:** reissued statements, ASC 855-10-25-4. Not taught anywhere in the platform's FAR lesson.
  - **far-se-08:** the content of the disclosure (nature and estimate of effect).
  - **far-se-06(d):** settlement of a tax dispute. Not taught, and contestable.
  - **far-se-19:** a dividend declared after year-end. Not in this lesson; far-equity teaches that declaration creates the liability, so it is derivable.
  - **far-se-20:** a tax rate enacted after year-end. Not in this lesson, but taught in far-income-taxes (an earlier module), so this is acceptable.
  - **far-se-09:** a lawsuit filed after year-end for a pre-YE event. Taught in far-contingencies (earlier module) and covered by this lesson's principle.
  - **far-se-07, far-se-17, far-se-18, far-x3-15(d):** theft, warranty claims data, embezzlement and the bonus determination. Not listed in the lesson, but they follow from the principle. Acceptable.
- **Sequencing.** The lesson relies on ASC 450 accrual (far-contingencies, the prior module in the same unit), on EPS (far-u2) and on deferred-tax enacted rates (far-u10). All come earlier, so there is no reliance on later modules.
